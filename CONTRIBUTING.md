# Contribution and Editorial Rules

Contributions should improve a concrete drawing-to-tool decision. A useful addition identifies the feature, material condition, tolerance or surface requirement, selected carbide geometry, holder/reach constraint, rejected alternative, toolpath implication and inspection method.

## Required standards

- Use original wording and cite external factual sources where needed.
- Label hypothetical examples; do not invent customers, confidential drawings or production results.
- Avoid vendor promotion unless a product specification is essential and verifiable.
- Do not present starting parameters as released values for an unknown machine or setup.
- Explain why the tool fits the feature instead of listing generic cutter types.
- Keep links contextual. Link directories, reciprocal-link schemes and keyword-stuffed anchors are not accepted.

## Suggested submission shape

1. Drawing feature and functional requirement
2. Material condition and dominant failure risk
3. Tool diameter, flute count, geometry, grade/coating and edge preparation
4. Reach and holder decision
5. Roughing, semifinishing or finishing role
6. Rejected alternative and reason
7. Toolpath and chip/burr control
8. Inspection gate

This repository is maintained as an educational technical reference and does not certify a manufacturing process.
